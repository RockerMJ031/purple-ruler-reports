What shipped last week

16–22 November 2026. Written from the user’s side: who sees what, on which page. Use the tabs for the full lists.

OverviewNew featuresBug fixesUnder the hood

In short

  • Extend now covers more of what schools actually renew: every A, C and E row has a coloured Extend button, Blueprint / Distinction groups are priced from their current members, and pupil-type renewals ask for what is missing and for contact details. The payer account card on Billing & Credits became visible on 22 Nov.
  • Add support works from the Suggested support card (including Evolve), and schools can remove a single pupil from a group directly, with a “Leaving” marker in group lists.
  • Billing & Credits has a new school page (one row per course, weekly credits for early leavers), a payer account card, and schools now see only tickets raised from the school dashboard.
  • Trust Overview gains a pupils table with Extend and a Latest welfare calls card; the Assign pupils button now appears for trust admins, and Credits used this month fills for every trust.
  • Students are asked for post-lesson feedback only for lessons they attended, and the prompt appears when the lesson ends (back end 11 Nov, page 14 and 18 Nov).
  • Call Centre: pupil search when adding a card, another-contact on manual cards, data colouring. Staff with a purpleruler.com login now land on the routing page.
  • 21 back-end releases (batches 209–230); the big back-end theme of the week was the daily billing run, split into segments and moved towards school-by-school batches (still shadow-only).
