School dashboard14

  • Extend on every pupil row that can be extended, in three coloursLive

    The Pupils table now shows a labelled Extend button on every A, C or E row that can be extended: green once lessons have stopped, light purple with 28 days or more left, amber inside 28 days. Pupils who stopped early (before the order ended) also appear in the ended list.

    The Extend button on a B / D group card now works out the quote from the pupils currently in the group, and the old per-order Extend entry on those rows is gone. Groups that run on alternate weeks are handled.

  • Extend for pupil-type orders: dialog asks for what is missingLive

    The extension dialog now builds the quote from the pupil's own details, asks for any missing items, and starts the new period the day after the previous one ends. Contact name, email and phone are asked every time, pre-filled and editable.
