Purple Ruler · Release notes · 9–15 Nov 2026

What shipped last week

9–15 November 2026. Written from the user’s side: who sees what, on which page. Use the tabs for the full lists.

OverviewNew featuresBug fixesUnder the hood

In short

  • Teachers and trust case workers now see only their own pupils, and school leads can assign pupils to teachers.
  • Extend is more flexible: A, C and E courses can be extended any time, and missing home/school details are asked for. The fix for the B/D course Extend error was merged in the week and released on Monday 16 Nov.
  • Home pages: a tidier school Overview, “Credits used this month” blocks, a Last lesson column, and a fix for the 60-second timeout on large schools.
  • Enrolment: a 13-subject list, lesson days and weeks under each pupil's dates, and clearer progress cards.
  • Call Centre: raise a ticket while logging a call, Welfare Lead manual requests, an Assigned to filter.
  • 27 back-end releases (batches 181–207); batch 208 followed on Monday 16 Nov.
