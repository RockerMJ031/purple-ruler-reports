School dashboard17

  • Teachers see only their own pupilsLive

    A school teacher now sees only the pupils assigned to them; the dashboard's pupil data follows the same limit.

    2026-11-09velo#2198 pages#1978
  • Assign pupils to a teacher can be savedLive

    School leads can save pupil-to-teacher assignments from Team, and the dashboard tells each user whether they are allowed to assign.

    2026-11-14velo#2209 velo#2334
  • Shared group list: click ENROLLED to see your school's pupilsLive

    In a shared group, the ENROLLED count can be clicked and lists only your own school's pupils.

    2026-11-09pages#1979
  • Re-enrolling a pupil into a group: year group and end dateLive

    When a school re-enrols a deactivated pupil into a group, it picks the year group (required) and can set an end date (optional). The request is now handled automatically.

    2026-11-11velo#2224 velo#2228 pages#2004
  • Add pupil: parent contact detailsLive

    The Add pupil dialog can take a parent's name, email and phone (all optional), for both a new pupil and an existing one. It is not shown when re-enrolling a deactivated pupil.

    2026-11-12velo#2256 pages#1999
  • Home page tidy-up: Get started card removed, welfare calls card movedLive

    The Get started card is gone and the content below moves up. The latest welfare calls card now sits under Tickets in the right-hand column, and clicking a call shows its details (up to 10 calls).

    2026-11-13velo#2321 pages#2017 pages#2028
  • Four small home page changesLive

    The subtitle reads “this academic year”, attendance below the threshold no longer shows “of N”, the weekly table is sorted by this week's attendance, and the A-002 block is hidden when a school has no groups.

    2026-11-12velo#2296 pages#2014
  • Pupils table: End column is now “Last lesson”Live

    The End column is renamed Last lesson (the values do not change); card labels, hover text and footnote follow.

    2026-11-15pages#2048
  • Notice when the pupil list is cut off at 1,000Live

    If a school's pupil list reaches the 1,000 limit, a notice above the list says it is truncated.

    2026-11-14velo#2347 pages#2046
  • Extend: courses A, C and E can be extended any timeLive

    The Extend dialog now has a start-date box and a note about gaps. The start date follows the original order and the school can choose it. The pupil page no longer limits this to 14 days, and the expiring card points schools to Extend.

    2026-11-14velo#2346 pages#2044
  • Extend: school fills in home / school when it is missingLive

    For older orders with no learning-from-home value, the Extend dialog asks the school to choose it. Works on the school and trust dashboards.

    2026-11-14velo#2341 pages#2041
  • Extend: clear message instead of a ticketLive

    Extending from the school or trust dashboard no longer raises a Course Extension ticket. If the order cannot be created, the dialog stays open and says so in one sentence.

    2026-11-13velo#2327 velo#2329 pages#2030
  • Weekly register: half-day tickLive

    A half-day cell now shows a tick if the pupil attended any lesson in it. Percentages are unchanged, and rows whose year group is Teacher are left out of attendance figures.

    2026-11-12velo#2303 velo#2310
  • Family timetables block is read-onlyLive

    Timetables that parents submit are listed read-only on School Home: no buttons, nothing to confirm.

    2026-11-13velo#2316 pages#2023
  • “Credits used this month” on Billing & CreditsLive

    Credit schools see how many credits were deducted this month, with a clear state when the figure is unavailable.

    2026-11-10velo#2234 velo#2235 pages#1994
  • Welfare calls to other contacts stay off the school dashboardLive

    Calls made to someone other than the parent are not shown as welfare calls on the home card, the lesson report page or the school calendar.

    2026-11-14velo#2351 velo#2353
  • Book a review meeting button matches Raise a ticketLive

    The top-bar button now has the same colour and shape as Raise a ticket.

    2026-11-14velo#2325 pages#2024

    Below a pupil's dates the page shows the number of lesson days and weeks. If it is not a whole week, the note turns yellow and suggests an end date that is.

Ops & internal tools13

  • Call Centre: raise a ticket while logging a callLive

    The log-a-call dialog has Raise ticket: pick a type, write a summary and save, in one step. A safety-type ticket that could not be sent shows a red banner. Re-saving a call whose ticket was already filed works.

    2026-11-12velo#2287 velo#2298 velo#2356 pages#2012
  • Call Centre: manual requests from a Welfare LeadLive

    A Welfare Lead can look up a pupil by email, confirm the name, pick an agent and add a note. The manual card shows who requested it and the note.

    2026-11-10velo#2261 pages#2001
  • Call Centre: Assigned to filterLive

    A new filter under the school dropdown shows only one person's cards, everyone except them, or Assignee unknown.

    2026-11-11pages#2008
  • Call Centre: Mine lane shows cards you can dialLive

    Mine holds cards assigned to you that can be dialled, plus the call you are on. Cards with no number, no link or awaiting review stay in their own lanes.

    2026-11-09velo#2214 pages#1983
  • Call Centre: longer last-call noteLive

    The last-call note on a card holds up to 500 characters instead of 80.

    2026-11-10velo#2219 pages#1990
  • Call Centre: manual card can carry another contact (back end)Live

    A Welfare Lead can attach another contact to a manual card and the agent can dial them in the page. The contact lives only on that card and is cleared after 30 days. Back end only so far.

    2026-11-15velo#2355
  • Faster sign-in redirectLive

    After sign-in, the loading screen no longer waits for its animation: the user is sent on as soon as the destination is known.

    2026-11-14velo#2348
  • Quote changes: Change Student ID accepts an emailLive

    A Change Student ID request can name the pupil by student ID or by email.

    2026-11-09velo#2223
  • Quote changes: undo a cancellationLive

    A cancelled order can be un-cancelled, and a cancel now remembers the state before it.

    2026-11-11velo#2271
  • Quote changes: term split and hours for Blueprint / Distinction ordersLive

    Term splits follow the order's timetable and the hours ordered; quote changes on B/D orders read hours from the order itself.

    2026-11-11velo#2267 velo#2277 velo#2280
  • Billing: weekly-order consumptionLive

    For weekly-paid orders the billing dashboard counts student-weeks to date times the weekly price (setup fee excluded), does not charge for over-purchase, and cancelled weekly orders count up to their last counted lesson.

    2026-11-10velo#2249 velo#2264
  • Billing: monthly modes use actual lessonsLive

    Orders invoiced monthly in arrears are billed on actual lessons without a cap at the order total, and Blueprint / Distinction prepayments are settled against actual lessons after the order's end month.

    2026-11-11velo#2268 velo#2273
  • Credit schools: submit card shows creditsLive

    The operations card for a credit order shows credits instead of an amount in GBP.
